## The entry

> Think of a web of thin wires spun round the whole earth, so that a letter, a voice, or a face may fly from any town to any other swifter than lightning.

One sentence, 152 characters. Every word in it was already in use before 1800.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It prints exactly the one sentence above, and nothing else.

## Why it fits the brief

The brief asks for a single sentence, at most 200 characters, that explains the
modern internet to a traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — using
no word coined after their time, no list of features, just a picture they could
actually hold in their head.

This sentence gives them exactly one picture: a web of thin wires spun round
the whole earth. A web is something an 1800 traveler knows — a spider's web, a
net of threads. Wires are things they know — thin drawn metal. And the three
things that travel along those wires are the three things the internet carries:
a letter (writing), a voice (speech), and a face (a likeness). The speed is
measured against the fastest thing they know: lightning.

Nothing in it is coined after 1800 — no internet, no computer, no screen, no
phone, no message sent through the air by means they could not picture. Just
wires, and things flying along them faster than lightning.
